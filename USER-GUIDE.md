# Using Bold7

BlackBerry OS 7 was built around a keyboard, a trackpad and a few keys, and most of what it can do is one key press away, but nothing on screen tells you so. This guide lists what Bold7 does today, screen by screen, so that nothing stays hidden. Everything here is what the current release does; where something from the 9900 is still missing, it says so.

If you used a 9900, most of it will come back to you. The official BlackBerry Bold 9900 user guide (OS 7.1) is still the best companion: Bold7 follows it, and this page cites it by page where it helps.

## The keys

There are no gestures in Bold7, as there were none on the 9900. Everything is done with these keys.

| Key | Press | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| **Menu** (BlackBerry logo, left of the trackpad) | The full menu of the screen: everything you can do there. | The app switcher (also **Switch Application**, first line of every full menu). |
| **Back** (the 9900's Escape) | Goes back one step: closes a menu, a dialog, a search, a folder, an app. | |
| **End** (red) | Ends a call. Otherwise returns to the home screen, drawer lowered. | The power-off dialog, when no call is in progress. |
| **Call** (green) | On the home screen, opens Phone. In Phone or Contacts, calls. Answers a ringing call. | |
| **Trackpad** | Moves the focus. There is no pointer. | |
| **Trackpad click** | Opens or chooses what has the focus. | The short menu: the few most useful actions, with **Full Menu** at the end. |
| **Alt** | The next key types its second character (digits and symbols printed on the keys). | Held, every key types its second character. |
| **Shift** | The next letter is a capital. | |
| **Sym** | The symbol list. | |
| **Space**, **Enter**, **Backspace** | As on any keyboard; many screens give them a job, listed below. | |
| **Power** | Turns the screen on or off. Turning it off locks the phone when a password is set. | The power-off dialog: **Full Power Off**, **Restart**, **Cancel**, and **Emergency** when Android offers it. |
| **Volume** | The volume window of OS 7, for the sound playing (or the call). | |

**Holding a letter key** as the first key on the home screen, in Messages or in Phone calls the number given to that letter for speed dial. **W** (the 1 key) calls your voicemail. A letter without a number asks whether to assign one.

**Digits**: the Q25 has no number row. Digits are on the letter keys, typed with Alt: W 1, E 2, R 3, S 4, D 5, F 6, Z 7, X 8, C 9, and 0 on its own key. Where only a number makes sense (the field of Phone, a PIN on the lock screen), the letter keys type digits without Alt.

## The home screen

### Banner, drawer and views

The top of the home screen is the banner: battery, date, network, the time, coverage, Wi-Fi. Under it, the indicator row: the speaker of the current sound profile on the left, then the counters of new items (email, text messages, missed calls, invitations, then other apps), and a magnifier. Move the focus up onto them with the trackpad:

| Click on | Opens |
|---|---|
| The banner | **Manage Connections** |
| The speaker | **Select a Profile** (sound profiles) |
| The counters | The notification list |
| The magnifier | Search |

At the bottom, the drawer holds the apps. Click its tab, or roll down from the row of icons, to raise it; click the tab again or press Back to lower it. Roll left or right on the tab to change the view, in a loop: **Frequent**, **All**, **Favourites**, **Media**, **Downloads**. Menu, **Manage Panels** chooses which views show (All always does).

On an icon, hold the trackpad click for its short menu:

- **Mark as Favourite** / **Unmark as Favourite**
- **Move** (in All and Favourites): the icon follows the trackpad, a click drops it, Back cancels.
- **Move to Folder**, when you have folders.
- **Hide Icon** / **Unhide Icon**. Hidden icons come back with Menu, **Show All**.
- **Launch**
- **Delete**, for apps you installed yourself. Android's own uninstall dialog confirms it.

The full menu (Menu) adds **Maximise Panel** / **Restore Panel**, **Add Folder**, **Manage Panels**, **Show All** and **Options**. A folder has its own short menu: **Open Folder**, **Edit Folder**, **Move**, **Delete Folder** (its apps go back where the folder was).

Each email account you add gets its own icon next to Messages, with a star when it has new mail; it opens Messages on that account alone.

The **Setup** icon opens the Setup Wizard again (text messages, wallpaper).

### Just type

On the home screen, start typing: search opens with what you typed. It finds apps, contacts, calls, text messages, calendar entries, pictures, music, videos, files and pages of Options or of Android's settings. Results come as tiles: apps first, then one tile per category with its count; click a category tile to see only that category, Back to leave it. **Extended Search** at the end sends the text to a Google web search in the browser.

Type a number (three digits or more) and a **Dial:** line appears first: click it, or press Call, and Phone opens with the number ready.

If you prefer the 9900's other way, Options, Display, **Home Screen Preferences**, **Launch by Typing**: **Application Shortcuts**. A letter then opens the first app whose name starts with it, and **H** opens Help.

### The notification list

Click the counters, or pull the banner down with a finger, and the list of notifications drops from the banner. Click a line to open it in its app; click a heading to open the app. Back closes the list. Android's floating notification cards are gone, except for calls, alarms and apps that need the whole screen.

### Manage Connections

**Mobile Network**, **Wi-Fi**, **Bluetooth** and **Near Field Communication**, each with a check box (only those the phone has). **All Off** turns everything off, then becomes **Restore**, which turns back on what was on. **Set Clock Alarm** opens Clock. **Options and Status** leads to **Networks and Connections** (Android's settings), **Wi-Fi Network** (Bold7's list of networks), **Service Status** and **Options**.

### Sound profiles

Click the speaker: **All Alerts Off**, **Normal**, **Loud**, **Medium**, **Silent**, **Vibrate Only**, **Phone Calls Only**. Normal, Loud and Medium ring at their own volumes; Vibrate Only vibrates; Silent keeps alarms and media only; Phone Calls Only lets calls ring and nothing else; All Alerts Off silences everything, the LED included. **Change Sounds and Alerts**, at the end of the list, opens the sound options.

### Switching apps

Hold Menu, or choose **Switch Application** at the top of any full menu. Roll to an app and click; Back or Menu closes the switcher. End always takes you home.

## The lock screen

Press the power key to turn the screen on. The lock screen shows the banner and the counters, as on the home screen.

If you set a password: swipe up on the screen, and the **Enter Password:** box opens. Type your password on the keyboard and press Enter. For a PIN, the letter keys type their digits without Alt (W 1, E 2, R 3, S 4, D 5, F 6, Z 7, X 8, C 9, and 0). Backspace erases; Back closes the box. After a wrong code: **Incorrect password!**, then Android's waiting time after too many attempts. **Emergency** opens the emergency dialer without unlocking.

Your code is always checked by Android itself: Bold7 only draws the box and hands the code over, without keeping it.

- To lock now, press the power key.
- To set, change or remove the password: Options, **Security**, **Password**: **Enable**, **Set Password** / **Change Password**, and **Lock After** (from 1 minute to 1 hour after the screen goes off). Bold7 offers passwords, as the 9900 did.
- To show your name and a phone number on the lock screen: Options, Display, **Message on Lock Screen**.
- While locked, nothing can be opened, answered or changed in Bold7's apps. An alarm of Clock rings over the lock screen; a Calendar reminder waits until you unlock.
- At start-up, the SIM PIN box (**Enter SIM PIN:**) comes before the phone's password.

## Messages

Messages is the unified inbox of the 9900: text messages, email, missed calls and the notifications of your other apps, newest first, grouped by day.

### Moving around the inbox

These are the 9900's shortcuts (user guide, pages 51 and 52). Letters work in capitals or not.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **C** | Compose an email (or a text message when you have no email account) |
| **T** / **B** | Top / bottom of the list |
| **Space** / **Shift + Space** | One screen down / up |
| **U** | Next unread message |
| **J** / **K** | Next / previous message from the same sender or number |
| **Alt + U** | Mark the message read or unread |
| **Alt + I** / **Alt + O** | Only incoming / only outgoing messages |
| **Alt + S** | Only text messages |
| **Alt + P** | Only missed calls |
| **Enter** or click | Open |
| **Shift + trackpad** up or down | Select several messages |
| **Back** | Step back: closes the folder, the selection, the search, the filtered view, then Messages |

Still missing from the 9900: W and Alt + F (flags), Alt + V, and the Delete key in the list.

### Menus

The full menu, on a message: **Open**, **Mark Read** / **Mark Unread**, **Save**, **Delete**. On a date heading: **Mark Prior Read** (everything up to and including that day) and **Delete Prior**. Then for every screen: **Compose Email**, **Compose Other** > **Text Message**, **Search**, **Search by** (**Sender** or **Recipient**, **Subject**), **Display Style** (**Group by Subject** or **Single Messages**), **View Folder**, **View Saved Messages**, **Message Notifiers**, **Options**, **Help**, **Close**.

- **View Folder** shows **All Messages**, **Incoming Messages**, **Outgoing Messages**, **Text Messages**, **Call Logs** (missed calls), **Unread Messages** or **Notifications**.
- **Save** keeps a message in **Saved Messages**; deleting it there only takes it off that list.
- **Search** puts a field at the top; it looks in senders, addresses, subjects and the start of each message. **Search by** fills it from the selected message.
- With several messages selected (Shift + trackpad), the menu offers **Mark Read**, **Mark Unread** and **Delete Messages**.
- Confirmations before Delete and Mark Prior Read can be turned off in Options.

### Notifications of other apps

What your other apps notify lands in Messages too. **Open** does what tapping the notification would do; **Mark Read** uses the app's own "mark as read" when it has one; **Delete** dismisses it. The main list keeps messaging and email apps; the others are in View Folder, **Notifications**.

### Text messages

Text messages go through Google Messages, which stays the SMS app of the phone and carries SMS, MMS and RCS. Bold7 draws the conversations. For that, Google Messages needs one setting: **Setup** on the home screen, **Text Messages**, **Open Google Messages**, then turn on **"Allow Bixby and text on Galaxy devices"**. Without it, opening or writing a text opens Google Messages instead.

In a conversation, type in the field at the bottom; **Enter** (or a click on the field) sends. The menu has **Send**, **Clear Field**, **Add Smiley**, **Attach** (**Picture**, **Video**, **Audio**, **File**; one attachment per message), **Open Attachment** and **Delete Message** on a bubble, **Clear Conversation**, and **Start Selection**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**. Roll right past the end of the text to reach the smiley button. Back with text in the field asks whether to discard it: texts are not kept as drafts.

To start a new conversation: **C** (without email account) or **Compose Other** > **Text Message**, then type a name or a number in **To:** and pick a suggestion, or Menu, **Choose Contact**.

A picture opened from a conversation can be saved with **Save Image** (into Pictures, Messages).

Google Messages offers no way to mark a text as unread, so **Mark Unread** on a text only changes Bold7's list.

### Email

To add an account, see **[Adding an email account](EMAIL-SETUP.md)**: Messages, Menu, **Options**, **Email Account Management**, **Internet Mail Account**.

Reading an email: **R** reply, **L** reply to all, **F** forward, **T** / **B** top and bottom, **Space** / **Shift + Space** one page. The menu adds **Mark Unread** and **Delete** (moved to the server's trash). Emails are shown as plain text: no images, no remote content, and links show their text only. Bold7 downloads the last 7 days of Inbox, Sent and Drafts.

Writing: **To:**, **Cc:**, **Subject:**, then the text; **Send Using:** appears when you have several accounts. Send with the send button at the top or Menu, **Send**; **Save Draft** keeps it on the phone. If sending fails, the email waits in Drafts. Your signature ("Sent from my BlackBerry® smartphone" unless you change it) is added when the email leaves: Email Account Management, the account, **Edit**.

Not yet in Bold7: attachments in email (reading and sending), Bcc, sending in HTML, opening the server's drafts, and acting on the server while offline (Mark Read and Delete then change only the phone's list).

### Messages Options

Menu, **Options**:
- **Message Display and Actions**: **Hide Sent Messages**, **Confirm Delete**, **Confirm Mark Prior Read**.
- **Inbox Management**: which email accounts and whether text messages show in the inbox (each account stays visible from its own icon).
- **Email Account Management**: add, edit or delete email accounts.
- **Text Messaging**: Google Messages' own settings.

Save with Menu, **Save**, or Back and answer **Changes made!**.

### New and unread

A message stays unread until you open it, but it stops being *new* once you have looked at the inbox. The red LED and the counters on the home screen show what is new. The red LED needs the **LED** option of the Messages and Email notifier on (Options, Sounds and Ringtones, Sounds for Selected Profile), and stays off in All Alerts Off. The LED is amber when the battery is low, and green in coverage if you turn on **LED Coverage Indicator** (Options, Display, Screen Display).

## Phone

The field at the top, **Find Contact or Dial a Number**, takes what you type: the letter keys type digits without Alt (Alt gives the letter, to find a contact by name). Typing filters the call log and adds matching contacts.

- **Call** calls the number typed, or the line with the focus. A click does the same.
- Hold a letter as the first key to call its speed dial number; **W** calls voicemail.
- Menu: **Call From Contacts**, **Call Voice Mail**, **View** > **Speed Dial List**, **Add Speed Dial**, **Add Pause**, **Add Wait**, **Phone Ringtones**, **Status**, **Options**. On a line of the call log: the contact's actions, **Delete**, and **Copy** for an unknown number.
- During a call, the buttons are **Speaker**, **Mute**, **Hold**, **Add Participant**; the menu has **Swap**, **Join**, **Split Call**, **Drop Call**, **New Call**, **Activate Bluetooth**, **Home Screen** and **End Call**. Digit keys send tones. Back leaves the call screen without ending the call; End ends it. The volume keys set the call volume.
- An incoming call: Call answers, End ignores, a click silences the ringer.

## Contacts

Your contacts are Android's: Bold7 shows and edits them, it does not copy them. Type to filter the list: the start of each word of a name is enough (`d m` finds Dexter Morgan), and accents do not matter. **New Contact** is the first line. **Call** calls the contact with the focus.

Menu: **View**, **Edit**, **Delete**, **Send Contact Card** (by email or text message), **New Contact**, and **Email**, **Call**, **Text** for the contact.

## Calendar

Calendar opens on today. Your events are Android's, so they follow your Google or other calendar account.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **D**, **W**, **M**, **A** | Day, Week, Month, Agenda view |
| **T** | Today |
| **G** | Go to a date |
| **C** | New appointment |
| **Space** / **Shift + Space** | Next / previous day, week or month |
| Trackpad | Moves by hour and day |
| Click | Opens the appointment, or creates one at that time |

In **Day view**, typing starts a new appointment at the hour with the focus (Quick Entry: Enter saves, Back cancels), so the letter shortcuts work from the other views; in Day view use Menu, **View**. The menu also has **New Alarm**, **New Task**, **Go to Today**, **Go to Date**, and **Accept**, **Tentative**, **Decline** on invitations. A reminder offers **Open**, **Dismiss** and **Snooze**.

## Clock

The clock page: a click sets the alarm. The menu: **Set Alarm**, **Bedside Mode**, **Stopwatch**, **Timer**, **Options**. In the **Set Alarm** box, roll the trackpad to change the hour, the minutes and **ON** / **WEEKDAYS** / **OFF**, and press Enter to save. When the alarm rings, even over the lock screen, **Snooze** has the focus and Back snoozes too; **Dismiss** stops it.

Stopwatch and timer: roll left or right to the button you want (**Start** / **Stop**, **Lap** or **Reset**) and click. Clock's Options choose the clock face, what happens while charging (**Display Clock**, **Enter Bedside Mode**), the alarm tone, volume, snooze time and vibration.

## Tasks

Click **Add Task:** to write one. Type to filter. Menu: **New**, **Open**, **Mark Completed**, **Mark in Progress**, **Delete**, **Delete Completed**, **Hide Completed**, **Options** (sort order, snooze, confirmations). Tasks with a reminder show **Task Reminder** at the time set.

## MemoPad

Click **New Memo:** to write one; type in the list to filter. In a memo, the menu has **Find**, **Clear Field**, **Save**, and the selection commands. Back asks to save your changes.

## Pictures and Camera

Pictures opens on **Camera**, **Camera Pictures** and **Picture Library**. **Camera** starts the camera: Bold7 Camera on LineageOS 24, BenOS Camera on BenOS.

In a picture, a click or **I** zooms in, **O** or Back zooms out (then closes). Roll left or right to the previous or next picture; once zoomed in, the trackpad moves around the picture. **L** turns it a quarter turn on screen. The menu has **View Slide Show**, **Delete**, **Set As Wallpaper**, **Assign To Contact** and **Send** > **Text Message**.

## Calculator

Type the digits with Alt (W 1, E 2...), or move over the keys on screen with the trackpad and click. **Enter** is =, **Backspace** erases, **X** multiplies, **C** clears, Back clears and then closes. The menu converts to and from metric units.

## Flashlight

A click, Enter or Space turns the torch on or off. It turns off when you leave the app.

## Help

**Help**, near the end of every app's full menu, opens the 9900's help on that app's topic, read from your 9900 file. Type to search it; click a link to follow it; Back goes back a page. The contents end with a Bold7 section: removing Bold7, root and safe mode, system updates, the Setup Wizard.

If you installed Bold7 with the BlackBerry Simulator file rather than the OS Loader, the 9900's help cannot be read from it, and Help shows the Bold7 section only.

## Typing

Typing is Bold7's input method for the physical keyboard. It never logs a key or a word, and reads nothing in password fields.

| To get | Do |
|---|---|
| A capital | Hold the letter, or press Shift then the letter. |
| A digit or symbol printed on a key | Alt then the key, or hold Alt. |
| CAPS lock | Alt + right Shift. Shift turns it off. |
| NUM lock (every key types its digit or symbol) | Alt + left Shift. Shift turns it off. |
| An accented letter | Hold the letter and roll the trackpad left or right through à á â, é è ê, ç, ñ, ö... |
| A symbol not printed on the keys | Sym: press the letter shown under the symbol, or roll and click. Sym again for the next page; Back closes. |
| A full stop | Space twice after a word. |
| In an email field | Space types @ first, then a dot. |

**Selecting, copying and pasting** from the keyboard, in text fields: Shift + trackpad selects; Shift + Backspace cuts; Alt + click copies; Shift + click pastes. In Bold7's own screens, the full menu also has **Start Selection**, **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste**.

**Word substitution** replaces as you press Space (for example `cest` becomes `c'est`, and the English list of the 9900 is read from your 9900 file). To undo one, press Backspace twice. Add your own in Options, **Typing and Language**, **Word Substitution**. A few codes insert the date (`%d`), the time (`%t`) or the battery level (`%b`).

**Word learning**: Typing remembers words it did not know, from the second time you type them. Unknown words are underlined. Turn it off, or delete what it learned, in Options, Typing and Language, **Typing**, **Advanced Style Options**.

**Direct or Predictive**: Options, Typing and Language, Typing, **Style**. In Predictive, up to three words appear: roll left or right, Enter takes the word with a space, a click takes it without; Space keeps what you typed.

French and English work together, without switching. The **Currency Key** ($, €, £, ¥) and the key tone are in Options, Typing and Language, **Keyboard**.

## Options

The Options icon opens the OS 7 options list. Each screen saves with Menu, **Save**, or Back and **Changes made!**.

- **Sounds and Ringtones**: **Phone Ringtone**, **Sounds for Selected Profile** (the phone, the notifier of Messages and Email with its LED, reminders), **Profile Management**; **Sounds for Contacts** opens Android's settings.
- **Networks and Connections**: **Manage Connections**, **Wi-Fi Network** (Bold7's list, to join a network), and Android's pages for the mobile network, Bluetooth and NFC.
- **Display**: **Date and Time**, **Home Screen Preferences** (wallpaper, Launch by Typing, the Downloads Folder where new apps land, the views of the drawer), **Screen Display** (brightness, timeout, **LED Coverage Indicator**), **Message on Lock Screen**.
- **Typing and Language**: **Language** (the display language), **Typing** (style and word learning), **Trackpad Sensitivity** (horizontal and vertical, 20 to 100, 70 by default), **Keyboard**, **Word Substitution**; **Custom Dictionary** and **Spell Check** open Android's settings.
- **Device**: **About**, **Storage**, **Battery**, **Text Messages Setup**, and **Advanced System Settings**, which opens all of Android's settings.
- **Call Management** opens Android's call settings.
- **Security**: **Password** (the phone's password and Lock After) and **Encryption**.
- **Accessibility**.

## What Bold7 changes, and how to go back

Bold7 is a KernelSU module. It does not modify any partition; it sets itself as home screen, phone app and input method, takes over the trackpad, the End key and the notification LED, applies the 9900's sounds, silences Android's interface sounds and, on LineageOS 24, sets the screen density so that one pixel of the 9900 is one pixel of the Q25.

To keep your previous home screen or phone app while using the rest of Bold7: **Setup** on the home screen, **What Changed**, which opens Android's Default apps. Typing is chosen again as input method at every start while Bold7 is installed; to type with another keyboard for good, Bold7 has to be removed.

For Android's own settings: Options, Device, **Advanced System Settings**. A swipe down from the top of the screen inside an app shows Android's quick settings.

**Removing Bold7**: KernelSU manager, **Modules**, Bold7, **Remove**, then restart and unlock. Bold7 gives back your home screen, phone app, keyboard, trackpad service, LED, settings and sounds, removes its apps, then removes itself.

What stays and what goes:
- **Stays**: your text messages (Google Messages keeps them), contacts, calendars, call log, pictures, your email on the server, and your `Bold7/icons/` folder.
- **Goes with Bold7's apps**: memos, tasks, Clock's alarms, speed dial numbers, Saved Messages, the words Typing learned, and the email accounts set up in Bold7. Android does not back these up. Copy what matters before removing Bold7.

## Troubleshooting

- **The phone does not finish starting**: press **Volume down** repeatedly while it starts. KernelSU starts in safe mode with every module off; remove Bold7 from the manager and restart.
- **The trackpad stops moving the focus**: restart the phone.
- **The lock screen box shows no asterisks when you type**: touch the box (away from Emergency). Android's own password entry comes back until the next restart, and you can unlock with it.
- **Texts do not appear in Messages, or open in Google Messages**: Setup, Text Messages, and check that "Allow Bixby and text on Galaxy devices" is on in Google Messages.
- **An email account stops receiving**: the provider probably refused the password (changed, or app password revoked), or, on a small host, renewed a certificate you had accepted. Delete the account in Email Account Management and add it again; see [Adding an email account](EMAIL-SETUP.md).
- **After a LineageOS update, Android's look is back**: root was lost with the update. Reinstall KernelSU to the inactive slot as the [LineageOS 24 guide](INSTALL-LINEAGEOS24.md#weekly-lineageos-updates-keep-root) explains.
- **A letter on the home screen opens search instead of an app**: Launch by Typing is set to Universal Search (Options, Display, Home Screen Preferences).
- **Anything else**: ask in the [Bold7 topic](https://discord.com/channels/1356921788038119556/1572002370160828446) on the official Zinwa Discord, or open an issue on https://github.com/codexofc/bold7/issues with your system (LineageOS 24 or BenOS) and what you saw.
