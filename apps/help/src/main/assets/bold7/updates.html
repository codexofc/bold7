Updates of your phone's system

An update of your phone's system (BenOS, or LineageOS) keeps Bold7 installed, as long as your phone keeps its root.

LineageOS updates every week, and each update replaces the part of the system that holds the root. After the update is installed, and before you restart, open the KernelSU manager and install it again on the inactive slot, as the KernelSU manager offers after an update. Without this, your phone starts without Bold7's module: the look of Android comes back, and Bold7's apps run without what the module gives them.

If something looks wrong after an update, install Bold7 again.
