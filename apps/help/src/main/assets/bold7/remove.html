Remove Bold7

Bold7 is a module of KernelSU. Removing it gives your phone back its own look.

  1. Open the KernelSU manager.
  2. In Module, find Bold7 and select Uninstall.
  3. Restart your phone.
  4. Unlock your phone. Bold7 puts back what it changed (home screen, phone and keyboard apps, sounds, settings) and removes its apps, then removes itself.

Your messages, contacts, calendar, pictures and calls stay on your phone: Bold7 shows them, it does not keep them.
