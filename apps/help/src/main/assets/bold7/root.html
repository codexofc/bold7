Root and safe mode

Bold7 needs root to install: KernelSU puts it in place each time your phone starts, without changing any partition of your phone. Bold7's apps never use root once installed.

If your phone does not finish starting after Bold7 is installed, start it in the safe mode of KernelSU: press the Volume Down key repeatedly while your phone starts. Every module, Bold7 included, is then left out, and you can remove Bold7 from the KernelSU manager.

Leave the other modules of your phone as they are: Bold7 does not need them changed.
