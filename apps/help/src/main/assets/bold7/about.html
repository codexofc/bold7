About Bold7

Bold7 gives your smartphone the look and feel of BlackBerry OS 7.1, as on the BlackBerry Bold 9900. It runs on Android: your phone keeps its own system, and Bold7 sits on top of it.

The pictures, sounds and fonts of BlackBerry OS 7, and the help you are reading, were taken from your own copy of the official file of the BlackBerry Bold 9900 when Bold7 was installed. Bold7 itself carries no file of BlackBerry.

The topics under Bold7 in the contents were written for Bold7. Everything else in Help is the help of the BlackBerry Bold 9900 as BlackBerry wrote it: some of it describes features your phone does not have.
